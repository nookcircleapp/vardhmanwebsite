Townships in Ayodhya Bypass.

The complete 2026 guide — what's launched, what's coming, infrastructure, prices, and which township fits your family.

Ten years ago, Ayodhya Bypass was farmland and a fuel pump. In 2026, it is Bhopal's most active township corridor — eleven master-planned projects in different stages of development, the Outer Ring Road interchange under construction, and prices that have tripled since 2018. This guide is what we tell our own friends asking, "Should I buy on Ayodhya Bypass?"

Why the corridor exists at all

Three things had to come together. First, the Madhya Pradesh government's Town & Country Planning Department approved the Bypass corridor for residential conversion in 2017, releasing 600+ acres for development. Second, road widening to 80–100 ft made the area drivable. Third, civic amenities — DPS Bhopal Bypass, Sagar Public School, Bansal Hospital, Jio Mart — moved in fast.

Result: by 2024, eleven townships were either launched or under approval. By 2026, three are delivered, four are nearing possession, and four more are mid-construction.

Infrastructure that's already there

  • Schools. DPS Bhopal (Bypass), Sagar Public School, Bansal Academy, Bhabha Academy — all within 4 km of most townships.
  • Hospitals. Bansal Hospital (15-minute drive), Aakash Hospital, Apollo Sage in planning.
  • Retail. Jio Mart, DMart announced, multiple supermarkets within townships.
  • Road. 80 ft to 100 ft wide approach roads on the spine. Service lanes operational. Storm water drainage upgraded in 2024.
  • Public transport. Auto stand, e-rickshaw service, two BCLL bus routes already serve the corridor.

Infrastructure that's coming

  • Outer Ring Road interchange — under construction, opening expected 2027. Will cut the drive to AIIMS Bhopal and the airport by 12–15 minutes.
  • BRTS extension (proposed). Would put a dedicated bus corridor along the spine.
  • 5-star hotel + super-specialty hospital in planning stages on the parcel adjacent to the bypass.
  • Metro Phase 2 — long-term, but the planning documents from BMRCL show two stations within 2 km of the corridor.

The honest counter-case

Ayodhya Bypass is not for everyone. If your work is at MP Nagar or you need to be near Bittan Market every day, the daily 25–35 minute drive will get old. Resale liquidity is still maturing — you can sell in 2 months on Hoshangabad Road, it may take 4–6 on Ayodhya Bypass right now. Restaurants and nightlife are still thin. The corridor is built for families who value space, gated living, and the upside curve — not for buyers who want city-centre walk-out access.

FAQ

Why are townships being built on Ayodhya Bypass?

State land conversion in 2017, wider roads, low historical land cost — the three ingredients that made master-planned townships viable here when Hoshangabad Road ran out of large land parcels.

What's the price range for 2/3/4 BHK on Ayodhya Bypass in 2026?

2 BHK ₹38–55 L, 3 BHK ₹55–85 L, 4 BHK / triplex ₹95 L–1.6 Cr. Premium addresses sit at the upper end.

Is Ayodhya Bypass a good investment for 2026?

Yes for a 3–5 year horizon — the Outer Ring Road interchange is the next big appreciation trigger. For 1-year horizons, Hoshangabad Road has better resale liquidity.

Which township fits families with school-age kids?

Within 4 km of DPS Bhopal (Bypass), Sagar Public School, or Bansal Academy. Celestia, Aakriti, Sagar Pearl all qualify.
