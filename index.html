Our Story · Chapter One

A quieter way
to build.

In 1992, we delivered Pulkit Apartments — our first project, in a city that was still finding its skyline. Thirty years on, ten projects later, the principles haven't moved. Premium materials. Honest construction. Wide internal roads. Modular kitchens. The kind of natural ventilation you only notice when it's missing somewhere else.

We don't build apartments. We build environments — and we build them for the families who'll outlast the buildings around them. From Pulkit in 1992 to Fairmont and Celestia today, the standard hasn't shifted.
