Our Story · Since 1992

Three decades, written into Bhopal.

From a single building in 1992 to nineteen addresses today — the Vardhman story is the story of a city growing up.

Chapter One · The Beginning

It started in 1992. With Pulkit.

When we delivered Pulkit Apartments in 1992, Bhopal looked different. Our first project was an act of conviction — a quiet belief that a home should be more than four walls. It should be a sanctuary. Thirty years and nineteen projects later, that belief hasn't moved.

Today, we are among Bhopal's most trusted names in real estate — known for premium residential and commercial properties that combine architectural elegance with thoughtful, sustainable engineering. Over two thousand five hundred families have made a Vardhman address their home. Each one is a story we hold close.
