Luxury Format

The 5 BHK triplex — Bhopal's new luxury format.

Why families who could afford anything are choosing vertical mansions over villas and apartments alike.

8 min read· Updated June 2026· By Vardhman Constructions·
  • A triplex is a single residence across three vertical floors with an internal staircase. 5 BHK distributes the bedrooms across the levels.
  • Apartment security + villa space. 3,500–5,500 sq ft per family in a gated society.
  • Price band: ₹1.6–3.2 Cr on Hoshangabad Road. ₹2.0–3.8 Cr in Shahpura. ₹2.4–4.2 Cr in Arera Colony.
  • Supply is constrained — fewer than 50 units across all of Bhopal in 2026.

Ten years ago, Bhopal's idea of luxury was a villa in Shahpura or a 3,500 sq ft apartment in MP Nagar. Today, a third option has overtaken both for premium families — the 5 BHK triplex. Three private levels, internal stairs, apartment-grade security, villa-grade space.

What is a 5 BHK triplex, exactly?

A typical layout:

  • Level 1 (ground): Drawing, dining, kitchen, store, servant quarter with bath, one bedroom. The "public" floor.
  • Level 2: Three bedrooms — typically master suite plus two children's bedrooms. The "family" floor.
  • Level 3: One bedroom + covered terrace + open terrace. Often used as study, guest room, or parent suite. The "private" floor.

You live in 3,500–5,500 sq ft of vertical space but enter through a single apartment-level main door, with apartment security and apartment-grade common amenities.

Why families are switching from villas

Triplex over villa
  • Security. Villa = 24/7 guard, alarm, perimeter walls. Triplex inside a gated society = institutional security included.
  • Maintenance. Villa = you fix everything. Triplex society has elevator, water, generator, common-area maintenance.
  • Vacation travel. Lock and leave a triplex for a month — house is safe. A villa is anxiety-inducing.
  • Resale liquidity. Premium triplexes sell faster than premium villas because the buyer pool is wider.

Why families are switching from apartments

Triplex over apartment
  • Privacy between generations. Grandparents on one floor, kids on another.
  • Servant quarters. Dedicated quarter with bath and kitchen access. 4 BHK apartments rarely include this.
  • Covered terraces. Outdoor space that's yours alone.
  • Skyline views. A 3rd-floor terrace is essentially a penthouse view.

The honest counter-case

Triplexes are not for everyone. If you live with aging parents who struggle with stairs, the format is hostile. Some triplexes offer an optional lift (significant additional cost).

Plan for
  • Cooling cost. 5,000 sq ft costs more to cool than 2,500 sq ft.
  • Furnishing capex. Budget ₹15–25 L for full interiors.
  • You can't expand later. Buy the right floor plan once.
