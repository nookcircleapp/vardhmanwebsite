Ten years ago, Ayodhya Bypass was farmland and a fuel pump. In 2026, it is Bhopal's most active township corridor — eleven master-planned projects in different stages, the Outer Ring Road interchange under construction, and prices that have tripled since 2018. This guide is what we tell our own friends asking, "Should I buy on Ayodhya Bypass?"

Why the corridor exists at all

Three things had to come together. First, the Madhya Pradesh government's Town & Country Planning Department approved the Bypass corridor for residential conversion in 2017, releasing 600+ acres for development. Second, road widening to 80–100 ft made the area drivable. Third, civic amenities — DPS Bhopal Bypass, Sagar Public School, Bansal Hospital, Jio Mart — moved in fast.

Infrastructure that's already there

On-ground today
  • Schools: DPS Bhopal (Bypass), Sagar Public School, Bansal Academy, Bhabha Academy — all within 4 km
  • Hospitals: Bansal Hospital (15-min drive), Aakash Hospital, Apollo Sage planned
  • Retail: Jio Mart, DMart announced, multiple supermarkets within townships
  • Road: 80–100 ft wide approaches, service lanes operational, storm drainage upgraded 2024
  • Transit: Auto stand, e-rickshaw service, two BCLL bus routes

Infrastructure that's coming

2027 catalysts
  • Outer Ring Road interchange — opens 2027. Cuts AIIMS Bhopal + airport drive by 12–15 min.
  • BRTS extension proposed along the spine
  • 5-star hotel + super-specialty hospital planned on adjacent parcel
  • Metro Phase 2 — long-term, two stations within 2 km in BMRCL plans

The honest counter-case

Ayodhya Bypass is not for everyone. If your work is at MP Nagar or you need Bittan Market every day, the 25–35 minute daily drive will get old. Resale liquidity is still maturing — you can sell in 2 months on Hoshangabad Road, it may take 4–6 on Ayodhya Bypass right now. Restaurants and nightlife are still thin.

Frequently asked

Why are townships being built on Ayodhya Bypass?

State land conversion in 2017, wider roads, low historical land cost — three ingredients that made master-planned townships viable when Hoshangabad Road ran out of large land parcels.

What's the price range for 2/3/4 BHK in 2026?

2 BHK ₹38–55 L · 3 BHK ₹55–85 L · 4 BHK/triplex ₹95 L–1.6 Cr. Premium addresses at upper end.

Is Ayodhya Bypass a good investment?

Yes for 3–5 year horizons — the Outer Ring Road interchange (2027) is the next big appreciation trigger. For 1-year horizons, Hoshangabad Road has better liquidity.

Which township fits families with school-age kids?

Within 4 km of DPS Bhopal (Bypass), Sagar Public School, or Bansal Academy. Celestia, Aakriti, Sagar Pearl all qualify.
