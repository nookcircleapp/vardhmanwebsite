The Project

A vertical mansion, on Bhopal's most-asked street.

Vardhman Fairmont sits 200 metres from the main Hoshangabad Road — directly opposite Nandan Palace, accessed by an 80-foot-wide road. The address is, simply, where Hoshangabad Road, Bhopal is going. Adjacent to Ubuntu Hospital, with a 5-star hotel and a super-specialty hospital coming up around the corner — every convenience you'd want is already moving in next door.

Inside, the homes are 5 BHK triplexes: three private levels for a single family, complete with servant quarters (their own kitchen and store), generous covered terraces, optional lift installations, and the kind of natural ventilation and skyline views that make a house feel like a vertical mansion.
