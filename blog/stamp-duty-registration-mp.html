Stamp duty in MP, 2026.

The ₹4–10 lakh expense most Bhopal buyers forget until possession day. Here's what to actually budget.

6 min read· Updated June 2026· By Vardhman Constructions·
  • Standard rate: 7.5% of property value (5% + 2.5% municipal).
  • Women buyers: 6.5% total — 1% concession that saves ₹40k–2L depending on flat size.
  • Registration fee: 3% capped at ₹30,000 (most premium flats hit the cap).
  • Budget rule: plan for 10% extra cash on top of flat price at possession.

Every week we meet first-time buyers who budgeted carefully for the flat price plus the loan, then got blindsided by the stamp duty bill at registration. ₹4 lakh on a ₹50 L flat, ₹9 lakh on a ₹1.2 Cr flat. Worth planning for upfront.

The women-buyer concession — actually use it

Easy ₹50k–2L saving

If the property is registered solely in the woman of the family's name, you save 1% — ₹40,000 to ₹2 lakh depending on flat size. Joint registration averages 7.0%, still saving 0.5%.

Note: the loan can still be in both names regardless of who's registered. Banks have no issue with this.

What value is stamp duty calculated on?

The higher of:

  • Agreement value — what you actually paid
  • Government guideline value — circle rate set by MP government for that locality

Most Bhopal premium flats sell at or above guideline value, so the agreement value applies. Older areas sometimes have legitimate market prices below circle rate — in which case you pay duty on the higher circle rate.

When you pay

Stamp duty + registration are paid at the time of property registration. Typically you draft a banker's cheque for the full amount in favour of the Sub-Registrar, Bhopal, and bring it to the registration office.

Cash flow note

Most home loans don't cover stamp duty — it's an out-of-pocket expense. Plan for it 60 days before possession.

Other one-time costs around registration

  • Legal/advocate fees: ₹15–30k
  • GST on under-construction: 5% on agreement value (1% if affordable housing)
  • Society maintenance deposit: 6–12 months upfront, ₹30k–₹2 L
  • Society membership and corpus fund: typical ₹50k–₹2 L
Rule of thumb: budget 10% extra cash on top of the flat price at possession for stamp duty, registration, society deposits, interiors, and movers. On a ₹75 L flat that's ₹7.5 L.

Frequently asked

What's the stamp duty rate in MP?

7.5% standard, 6.5% for women buyers. Effective ~7.6–7.8% including registration fee.

Registration fee?

3% of value, capped at ₹30,000. Premium flats hit the cap.

Do women get a discount?

Yes — 1% concession (6.5% total). Saves ₹40k–2L.

On what value is duty calculated?

Higher of agreement value or government guideline (circle) rate.
