The Project

Four homes. One address.

Celestia is what we'd build if we were designing for ourselves — a community of premium homes in four distinct configurations, so a young couple, a growing family, and parents-in-the-house all find the layout that suits them. 2 BHK, 3 BHK Type A, 3 BHK Type B, and a flagship luxury triplex — all carrying the same Vardhman build standard and the same calm, well-planned community around them.

On Ayodhya Bypass, Celestia sits on what's becoming one of Bhopal's most-asked-after residential corridors. Modular-ready kitchens. Premium fittings. Generous balconies. The kind of natural light and ventilation that makes a home, a home. Pre-approved home loans from leading banks mean the paperwork stays out of your way.
