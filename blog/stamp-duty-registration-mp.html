Stamp duty in MP, 2026.

The ₹4-10 lakh expense most Bhopal buyers forget until possession day. Here's what to actually budget.

Every week we meet first-time buyers who budgeted carefully for the flat price + the loan, then got blindsided by the stamp duty bill at registration. ₹4 lakh on a ₹50 L flat, ₹9 lakh on a ₹1.2 Cr flat. Worth planning for upfront.

The actual rates (Madhya Pradesh, 2026)

Real numbers for typical Bhopal flats

  • 2 BHK at ₹48 L → standard ₹3.6 L | women buyer ₹3.1 L (saves ₹50,000)
  • 3 BHK at ₹75 L → standard ₹5.6 L | women buyer ₹4.9 L (saves ₹75,000)
  • 4 BHK at ₹1.2 Cr → standard ₹9.0 L | women buyer ₹7.8 L (saves ₹1.2 L)
  • 5 BHK triplex at ₹2 Cr → standard ₹15 L | women buyer ₹13 L (saves ₹2 L)

Numbers add registration fee (effectively ₹30k for premium flats — at the cap).

The women-buyer concession — actually use it

If the property is registered solely in the woman of the family's name, you save 1% — which is anywhere from ₹40,000 to ₹2 lakh depending on flat size. This is a genuine saving the government created to encourage women's property ownership. Joint registration (both spouses) averages to about 7.0%, still saving 0.5%.

Note: the loan can still be in both names regardless of who the registered owner is. Banks have no issue with this. Talk to a CA if you want to optimise for tax + ownership.

What value is stamp duty calculated on?

The higher of:

  • Agreement value — what you actually paid, as per the registered sale deed
  • Government guideline value — the circle rate set by MP government for that locality

Most Bhopal premium flats sell at or above the guideline value, so the agreement value is what matters. Older areas (heritage Bhopal, parts of MP Nagar) sometimes have legitimate market prices below circle rate — in which case you pay duty on the higher circle rate.

When you pay

Stamp duty + registration are paid at the time of property registration — which is also when possession is handed over for ready-possession flats, or at agreement-to-sell for under-construction flats. Typically you draft a banker's cheque for the full amount in favour of the Sub-Registrar, Bhopal, and bring it to the registration office.

Plan for cash flow accordingly. Most loans don't cover stamp duty — it's an out-of-pocket expense.

Other one-time costs around registration

  • Legal/advocate fees: ₹15-30k for full deed drafting and due diligence (optional but recommended; some builders provide a basic deed but a personal advocate review is worth it)
  • GST on under-construction: 5% on the agreement value (1% if affordable housing). Already in the flat price for most projects but verify.
  • Society maintenance deposit: usually 6-12 months upfront, ranges ₹30k-₹2 L
  • Society membership and corpus fund: typical ₹50k-₹2 L

Budget rule of thumb

On top of the flat price, plan for ~10% extra cash at possession to cover stamp duty, registration, society deposits, interiors, and movers. On a ₹75 L flat that's ₹7.5 L. Better to plan for it than be surprised.

FAQ

What's the stamp duty rate in MP?

7.5% standard, 6.5% for women buyers. Effective ~7.6-7.8% including registration fee.

Registration fee?

3% of value, capped at ₹30,000. Premium flats hit the cap.

Do women get a discount?

Yes — 1% concession (6.5% total). Saves ₹40k-2L depending on flat price.

On what value is duty calculated?

Higher of agreement value or government guideline (circle) rate.
