First home in Bhopal.

The complete checklist — from setting budget to picking up keys. Written by a Bhopal builder family.

Buying a first home in Bhopal is bigger than the price tag. The decisions you make in the first month — what locality, what builder, what configuration — determine the next 10 years of your daily life. This is the full process, in the order we have walked dozens of first-time buyers through.

Phase 1 — Set the budget (Week 1)

  1. Calculate your loan eligibility. Banks lend roughly 56-58x your monthly take-home for EMI. ₹1 L net salary → ~₹65-72 L loan at 8.75% over 20 years. See our home loan guide for details.
  2. Add 20% down payment in cash. Total budget = loan amount + down payment.
  3. Reserve 10% extra cash for stamp duty, registration, society deposits, interiors, and moving. On a ₹75 L flat, that's ₹7.5 L over and above the price.
  4. Sanity check. If the EMI on your target flat is more than 45% of net take-home, scale back. 35-40% is comfortable.

Phase 2 — Pick the locality (Week 2)

This is the single highest-leverage decision. We have written two long pieces on this:

  • Hoshangabad Road vs Ayodhya Bypass — the side-by-side comparison
  • Townships in Ayodhya Bypass — if you're leaning toward Bypass

Short version: pick by commute time to your office, school distance for kids (if any), and 5-year appreciation outlook.

Phase 3 — Shortlist builders (Week 3-4)

Our complete guide is here: Best builders in Bhopal — 2026 buyer's guide. The 8-signal checklist:

  • Years in Bhopal (10+ is the baseline)
  • Delivered project count (not launched)
  • On-time delivery % on last 5 projects
  • RERA registration and complaint count
  • Existing owner testimonials from past buildings
  • After-sales service quality
  • Build quality you can audit on a 5-year-old project
  • Bank pre-approvals from 2+ nationalised banks

Phase 4 — Site visits + RERA check (Week 4-5)

  1. Visit 3-5 shortlisted projects. Don't book on the first visit.
  2. For each project, verify on rera.mp.gov.in. Step-by-step in our RERA verification guide.
  3. Walk through the sample flat AND the actual under-construction tower. They should match.
  4. Drive past one of the builder's 5-year-old projects. Look at the lobby, basement, lift maintenance. This tells you what your project will look like in 5 years.
  5. Get the floor plan in writing. Verify carpet area against RERA-registered numbers.

Phase 5 — Get loan pre-approval (Week 5-6, parallel)

Apply at 2-3 banks for in-principle approval. This is free and takes about a week. You get a letter saying "you are eligible for ₹X lakh subject to property due diligence." This lets you book confidently and gives you negotiation leverage with the builder.

Phase 6 — Book (Week 6-7)

  1. Negotiate. Most builders have 1-3% headroom. Ask for it.
  2. Get a booking receipt with the unit number, floor, agreed price, and payment plan.
  3. Sign the agreement-to-sell. Read it carefully — possession date, penalty clauses, what amenities are included.
  4. Pay booking amount only by cheque to the project's RERA-registered bank account. Never to a personal or company general account.

Phase 7 — Loan disbursement (Month 2-3)

Submit property documents to your chosen bank. Legal + technical clearance takes 5-15 days. Loan sanction letter issued. Sign loan agreement. Bank disburses in tranches as construction progresses (for under-construction) or full amount at registration (for ready possession).

During construction, you pay pre-EMI (interest only) on the disbursed amount.

Phase 8 — Possession + registration (Month 18-36)

  1. Builder sends a possession notice 30 days in advance.
  2. Do a snag list inspection. Get everything fixed before taking handover.
  3. Pay stamp duty + registration fee at the Sub-Registrar's office. See our stamp duty guide.
  4. Sign the registered sale deed. This is when ownership actually transfers.
  5. Pay society maintenance deposit and corpus fund.
  6. Collect keys. Welcome home.

Common first-timer mistakes — avoid these

  • Booking before pre-approval. Means your loan might fall through and you lose the booking amount.
  • Paying to builder's general account. RERA mandates project-specific escrow. Verify the account.
  • Not budgeting for stamp duty + interiors. ₹5-15 L extra on top of price, depending on flat size.
  • Skipping site visits. Sample flat ≠ actual flat. Visit the under-construction tower.
  • Ignoring the 5-year-old project test. Builder's old project tells you the future of yours.
  • Choosing builder before locality. Locality drives 60% of the buy-vs-not decision; builder drives 30%.
  • Hot booking under pressure. "Today's price" is almost never today-only. Walk away if pressured.
