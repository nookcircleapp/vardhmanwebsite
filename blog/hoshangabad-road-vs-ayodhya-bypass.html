Locality Comparison

Hoshangabad Road vs Ayodhya Bypass.

Bhopal's two hottest corridors, side by side. Which one is right for your family?

11 min read· Updated June 2026· By Vardhman Constructions·
  • Hoshangabad Road for ready possession, mature infra, central access, and resale liquidity (2–3 months).
  • Ayodhya Bypass for higher appreciation (14–18% vs 8–10%), township-scale projects, and more space per rupee.
  • Price delta: Hoshangabad Road premium is ₹15–25 L higher for an equivalent 3 BHK.
  • ORR interchange opens 2027 — biggest single trigger for Ayodhya Bypass appreciation.

Every other home-buying conversation in Bhopal in 2026 starts with the same question: Hoshangabad Road or Ayodhya Bypass? We build on both. Vardhman Fairmont sits 200 metres off Hoshangabad Road. Vardhman Celestia is on Ayodhya Bypass. So we have no incentive to push you either way — and a clear view of who fits where.

The 30-second answer

Pick Hoshangabad Road if you want ready possession, your office is in central Bhopal, your kids already attend a Hoshangabad Road school, you value resale liquidity, and you can pay ~15–20% more per sq ft for the location premium.

Pick Ayodhya Bypass if you have a 3–5 year horizon, want more space for the money, are comfortable with under-construction possession, value township-scale living, and want to ride the ORR appreciation curve.

Connectivity

Hoshangabad Road connects to Habibganj station (12 min), MP Nagar (15 min), Bhopal Junction (22 min), airport (35 min). Two flyovers operational since 2019. Dense Ola/Uber coverage.

Ayodhya Bypass sits on Bhopal's outer arc, linking AB Road to Bhopal Bypass. The Outer Ring Road interchange (under construction, opening 2027) cuts the airport drive to under 22 minutes and connects directly to AIIMS Bhopal.

Schools

Hoshangabad Road catchment

Carmel Convent · Bhabha Higher Secondary · Sagar Public School (HR) · Delhi Public School (HR) · Indus World School. All within 5–7 km of any premium project.

Ayodhya Bypass catchment

DPS Bhopal (Bypass) · Sagar Public School (Bypass) · Bansal Academy · Bhabha Academy. Solid options but smaller catchment. If your child is at a Hoshangabad Road school today, factor the cross-city commute.

Hospitals

Hoshangabad Road: Ubuntu Hospital, Aakash Hospital, Bansal (~10 min), Apollo Sage planned. AIIMS Bhopal 20 min.

Ayodhya Bypass: Bansal (~12 min), Aakash (~15 min), super-specialty hospital announced for 2027. AIIMS Bhopal 18–22 min once ORR opens.

Retail & lifestyle

Hoshangabad Road: DB Mall, Aashima Mall, multiplexes, restaurants, salons — Friday-night plans don't require planning.

Ayodhya Bypass: Jio Mart, DMart confirmed. Restaurants mostly within township clubhouses. By 2027 this will look very different.

Hoshangabad Road is the safer bet. Capital preserved, 8–10% appreciation reliable, fast resale. Good for end-users who don't plan to move for 10+ years.

Ayodhya Bypass is the higher-beta bet. Multi-year appreciation wave driven by ORR, super-specialty hospital, township completions. 2018 buyers saw their flats triple in value by 2024.

Who should pick which

Pick Hoshangabad Road if you are…

  • A working professional with office in central Bhopal
  • A family with kids already in HR-area schools
  • A buyer with ready-possession requirements (move in 6 months)
  • A retiree wanting walking-distance hospitals + markets
  • Prioritising resale liquidity over peak appreciation

Pick Ayodhya Bypass if you are…

  • A 3–5 year horizon buyer comfortable with under-construction risk
  • A family wanting township-scale living (clubhouse, pool, gated)
  • A buyer maximising sq ft per rupee
  • An investor seeking the highest appreciation in the city
  • Working at AIIMS Bhopal, Mandideep, or along the outer arc
Two projects, two corridors. Fairmont on Hoshangabad Road — 5 BHK triplex. Celestia on Ayodhya Bypass — 2/3/4 BHK township. Same builder, same standards, different locations for different lives.

Frequently asked

Which is better — Hoshangabad Road or Ayodhya Bypass?

Depends on horizon and lifestyle. HR for liquidity + mature infra. Bypass for appreciation + space.

Is Hoshangabad Road more expensive?

Yes — typically ₹15–25 L more for an equivalent 3 BHK.

Which has better appreciation potential?

Ayodhya Bypass — 14–18% historical vs 8–10%. ORR (2027) is the next trigger.

Which is better for families with school-going children?

Both work. Pick on commute time from your specific project to your specific school — not on neighbourhood reputation.
