Locality · Bhopal

Bawadiya Kalan, Bhopal.

Township-scale living for families who want a city-inside-a-city — adjacent to Ayodhya Bypass where Vardhman Celestia is the standout 2/3/4 BHK address in 2026.

6 min read· Updated June 2026· Vardhman Constructions·

Bawadiya Kalan, in south Bhopal, is the city's original township belt. The area matured through the 2010s — schools, hospitals, retail and a steady resale market. If you want a city-inside-a-city lifestyle and don't mind being ~30 minutes from central Bhopal, this is the proven side of town. The premium new-build action today sits just next door on Ayodhya Bypass, where Vardhman Celestia is being delivered.

What's inside the belt

  • Township-scale developments with on-site schools, retail and hospitals.
  • Premium new-build action sits on the adjacent Ayodhya Bypass — Vardhman Celestia being the standout 2/3/4 BHK address in 2026.
  • Independent plots in surrounding pockets — for buyers wanting to build their own.
  • Mature resale market with multi-phase developments from the 2010s.

Schools and hospitals

  • Within the belt: on-site schools and a hospital in the larger township complexes
  • Within 4-6 km: leading CBSE schools, Bansal Hospital, Bansal Academy
  • Within 15-20 min drive: DPS Bhopal, Bhabha Higher Secondary, AIIMS Bhopal

Connectivity

  • 10-12 min to Hoshangabad Road junction
  • 20-25 min to MP Nagar
  • 30-35 min to Habibganj station
  • 35-40 min to Raja Bhoj Airport
  • BCLL bus connectivity decent; cab availability has improved meaningfully since 2022

The honest counter-case

Bawadiya Kalan's strength — township-scale, self-contained living — is also its limitation. The daily city-centre commute is longer than Hoshangabad Road or Arera Colony. If your work is in MP Nagar or central Bhopal and you commute by car, you're adding 30-40 minutes per day.

Who Bawadiya Kalan is right for

  • Families wanting school + retail + hospital inside the development
  • Joint families with multiple income earners where commute time is averaged across the family
  • Buyers prioritising weekend lifestyle over weekday convenience
  • Investors maximising sq ft per rupee in a township format
  • WFH professionals who only commute 1-2 days/week
